Your Post-Study Work Visa (PSWV)

Application fee: NZD $1,670 (non-refundable)

Processing time: Typically 2–5 weeks (80% within 5 weeks)

Work rights: Open work rights = any employer, any job

Application window: Within 3 months of your student visa expiry

Important: You can only get a PSWV once in your lifetime

How to Apply Online (Step by Step)

You do NOT need a consultant or immigration adviser. Follow these steps:

Step 1

Go to immigration.govt.nz — Post-Study Work Visa page

Step 2

Click 'Apply online' and log in with your RealMe account

Step 3

Fill in the application form. Enter all details exactly as they appear on your passport.

Step 4

Upload all documents as PDF files (max 10MB each).

Step 5

Pay NZD $1,670 and submit.

Step 6

Wait for processing (typically 2–5 weeks). Log in to check status. INZ will email if they need more docs.

Step 7

If student visa expires while waiting, INZ will normally issue an Interim Visa.
